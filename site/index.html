About Us

The Hanyang Database Lab was founded by Prof. Dong-Ho Lee to conduct scientific research in the field of knowledge and information processing and analysis. Our current focus spans digital healthcare, multimodal data analysis, de-identification of sensitive information, and physical AI. In digital healthcare, we develop AI-driven diagnostic systems, LLM-powered medical chatbots, and pose correction technologies for sports and rehabilitation. We explore deep learning-based audio representation, including multimodal retrieval and audio signal compression. For multi-object tracking, we design robust detection and tracking algorithms, privacy-preserving image/video processing, and advanced face and person tracking in crowded scenes. In the field of robotics, we advance SLAM-based autonomous driving using visual markers. In the field of big data, we optimize vector storage and retrieval for high-dimensional data, along with semantic multi-document summarization. Our recent publications address multimodal recommendation, medical AI, motion analysis, privacy protection, and autonomous navigation, aiming to bridge theoretical AI research with impactful real-world applications.
