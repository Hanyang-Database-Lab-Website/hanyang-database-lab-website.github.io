Research Topics

01Reasoning and Alignment in Generative AI and LLMs

Improved LLM reasoning and output alignment with user intent and factual information

  • Prediction-Augmented Generation (PAG)Integration of task-specific predictive models and LLM knowledge for classification and decision-making
  • Knowledge augmentation and value alignmentReduction of hallucinations and bias through knowledge augmentation and output alignment

02Multimodal AI and Intelligent Recommender Systems

Joint understanding and alignment of visual, acoustic, and textual data

  • Vision-text alignment for recommendationSemantic relationship learning between textual attributes and visual features for multimodal recommendation
  • Media analytics and object trackingHuman pose refinement, explainable embedding-based posture analysis, and multi-face video tracking
  • Audio enhancement and generationFine-to-coarse quantization for reduced regularization loss, improved modeling of music and environmental sounds, and extension to generative audio

03Medical AI and Healthcare Convergence

Symptom-to-disease inference and prediction using generative AI and LLM alignment

  • Symptom-to-disease inferenceMultimodal ensemble models and LLM reasoning using diverse symptom information
  • Knowledge-augmented diagnostic supportApplication of knowledge augmentation, value alignment, and PAG to medical data and diagnostic assistance

04AI-Based Security and Visual Processing

Privacy protection and de-identification through deep learning-based visual analysis

  • Context-adaptive de-identificationAdaptive privacy protection based on visual context and application requirements
  • Multi-face tracking and de-identificationMulti-face video tracking and real-time de-identification
