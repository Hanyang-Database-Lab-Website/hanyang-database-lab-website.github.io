Health & Human Performance

Digital Healthcare

  • Automatic Diagnosis System (ADS) based on natural language
  • AI doctor chatbot answering like human with Large Language Model (LLM)
  • Automatic pose correction for sports

Our Research

We study how predictive models, language models, and visual information can support healthcare and human performance. Our published work covers prediction-augmented diagnosis, patient-specific medication recommendation, and explainable golf swing analysis.

  • Integrating task-specific predictive models with language model reasoning.
  • Modeling relationships among diagnoses, procedures, and medication choices.
  • Refining human pose estimates and explaining differences in sports movements.

Our Publications

Featured research Findings of ACL 2025

Prediction-Augmented Generation for Automatic Diagnosis Tasks

Chan-Yang Ju and Dong-Ho Lee

Abstract summary

Prediction-Augmented Generation (PAG) connects task-specific predictive models with large language models for automatic diagnosis. Rather than relying only on next-token generation or copying an external model’s output, PAG uses predictive results together with language-model knowledge to refine its reasoning. The paper evaluates this approach across diagnosis datasets and introduces a way to assess generated answers in label-based prediction tasks.

Computer Vision & Visual Tracking

Multi-Object Tracking

  • Robust object detection and efficient tracking algorithm
  • Personal information de-identification on image or video sequences
  • Face/Person tracking on crowded scenes

Our Research

We investigate robust visual tracking in challenging videos. Our BoT-FaceSORT work combines shot change detection, shared feature memory, and adaptive matching to maintain face identities across frames with abrupt transitions and appearance changes.

  • Robust face tracking in unconstrained movie and web video sequences.
  • Detection and association under occlusion and appearance variation.
  • Privacy-aware visual analysis, including selective face de-identification.

Our Publications

Featured research ACCV 2024

BoT-FaceSORT: Bag-of-Tricks for Robust Multi-Face Tracking in Unconstrained Videos

Jonghyeon Kim, Chan-Yang Ju, Gun-Woo Kim, and Dong-Ho Lee

Abstract summary

BoT-FaceSORT addresses face tracking in videos with abrupt scene changes, occlusions, and changing appearances. It combines shot-change detection, shared feature memory, and adaptive cascade matching to maintain identities across frames. The work also introduces MovieShot, a benchmark built from challenging movie clips, and evaluates the contribution of each component across three tracking benchmarks.

Robotics & Applied AI

  • Explainable LLM on Robotics through Thought-Cloning
  • RoboNLP: Integrate NLP into Robots
  • Simultaneous Localization And Mapping (SLAM) based autonomous driving

Our Research

Our research interests include language integration in robotics and autonomous navigation. Our published SLAM work studies relative coordinate inference using monocular cameras, distance estimation, and trilateration in dynamic environments.

  • Relative localization with accessible camera-based sensing.
  • Visual understanding in environments containing moving objects.
  • Research directions in NLP-integrated robotics and explainable language reasoning.

Our Publications

Featured research ICTC 2023

Efficient Relative Coordinate Inference for Dynamic SLAM Exploiting Monocular Cameras

Seung-Chan Yu, Ji-Sung Park, and Dong-Ho Lee

Abstract summary

This work studies relative position inference for robots operating in dynamic environments. It combines camera-based distance measurement with trilateration, using fixed reference elements to estimate the camera’s location. The approach is designed to work with monocular cameras and explores a lower-cost alternative to sensing setups that require more extensive hardware or computation.

Multimodal Learning & Recommendation

Multimodal Representation Learning & Recommendation

  • Attention-guided visual-textual representation learning
  • Fine-grained cross-modal feature alignment
  • Efficient and personalized multimodal recommendation

Our Research

We investigate how visual and textual information can be aligned to improve personalized recommendation. Our recent MambaRec work combines multi-scale attention, local feature alignment, and global distribution regularization. This research connects multimodal representation learning with efficient information access.

  • Learning consistent representations across visual and textual modalities.
  • Combining local semantic alignment with global distribution regularization.
  • Balancing recommendation quality with representation efficiency.

Our Publications

Featured research CIKM 2025

Modality Alignment with Multi-scale Bilateral Attention for Multimodal Recommendation

Kelin Ren, Chan-Yang Ju, and Dong-Ho Lee

Abstract summary

MambaRec learns how visual and textual item features can work together in recommendation. Its attention-guided framework aligns fine-grained features locally and regularizes modality distributions globally. A dimensionality-reduction strategy also addresses the cost of high-dimensional representations. The paper evaluates the combined design on real-world e-commerce datasets, focusing on recommendation quality, generalization, and efficiency.
